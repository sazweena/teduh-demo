# Teduh (Cool Routes)

Team Simelarps, Township of Tomorrow (Planet), Sime Darby Property University Hackathon 2026.

Teduh helps township planners inspect walking heat, compare fixes within a budget, and hear from residents. 

## Open the planning workspace

- Map-first planning workspace: https://sazweena.github.io/teduh-demo/
- Full Elmina street map: https://sazweena.github.io/teduh-demo/map.html
- 3D Elmina planning view: [map-3d.html](map-3d.html)
- Resident report in the owner-app view: https://sazweena.github.io/teduh-demo/sdp-app.html?report=1
- Proposed owner-app integration: https://sazweena.github.io/teduh-demo/sdp-app.html

The homepage opens the map-first workspace at noon. Select a mapped street to inspect its hourly priority, set a budget, and save the included works under *Proposed changes*. Open *Reports* to review browser-local reports and the two entries carried over from the earlier planner. The resident phone view is available through the owner-app link above; its new reports currently stay within that page until reload.

## Data and limits

The map uses an OpenStreetMap snapshot for actual Elmina street geometry. The colored planning route uses nearby mapped *Jalan Elmina Ilham 3* as a visual reference for the script's *Jalan Elmina 3*. Confirm the exact pilot route and walking access on site. Priority, shade, walkers, fix costs, and impact are scenario estimates, not field measurements.

The map-first view uses MapLibre with OpenStreetMap raster tiles. It opens on the park footpaths beside Jalan Frekuensi U16/116 and U16/119, and can tilt to a 2.5D view on the same page. The building layer now covers the Ilham and Frekuensi area. It uses mapped OpenStreetMap building polygons where available. The original 16 Frekuensi blocks beside U16/137 retain their roof inset margins: 8 m at the north edge of the north row, 3 m at its south edge, 3 m at the north edge of the south row, 2.5 m at its south edge, and 1.5 m at each side. Larger mapped blocks are divided into smaller terrace roof units. Where OSM has no individual house outlines around Jalan Elmina Ilham 3, `v2-houses.mjs` places approximate 6.7 m by 11 m roof units beside residential road centre lines. The rows and developed side of outer streets were chosen using the user-provided 9 November 2026 aerial screenshot; the map coordinates come from OSM road geometry, not from surveyed or georeferenced roof tracing. Generated roofs are deliberately omitted near mapped building polygons and road junctions. These shapes and their uniform 6 m height are for map visualization, not verified building locations or heights.

The ground-shadow layer uses NOAA's solar position equations for the current Kuala Lumpur date, selected hour, and Elmina coordinates in Malaysia time (UTC+8), then projects each roof onto a flat ground plane. Neighbouring shadow polygons can overlap and visually darken in the map renderer. It computes direct-sun building shadows only: tree canopies, overhangs, roof profiles, terrain, clouds, and buildings outside the selected area are not included. The mapped roads are centre lines; this layer does not measure shade on surveyed sidewalks or calculate a pedestrian exposure score. Solar method: [NOAA General Solar Position Calculations](https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/solareqns.PDF).
The v2 street-priority overlay covers mapped residential streets and pedestrian links around Jalan Elmina Ilham 3. Its street lines come from a separate OpenStreetMap API extract dated 9 November 2026 (`v2-ilham-roads.json`), which includes Jalan Frekuensi U16/120, U16/121, and other streets missing from the older whole-town extract. Pedestrian links omitted at the north edge of that extract are supplemented by OSM footways in the 21 October 2026 whole-town extract (`elmina-map.json`), including the path through the strip beside Jalan Frekuensi U16/138. Mapped pedestrian paths have a blue line so they can be distinguished from street centre lines. Its 7 AM to 7 PM colours come from explicit planning weights in `v2-street-priority.mjs`, with Ilham 3 highest at 1 PM. These colours are relative planning priorities, not measured temperatures, shade percentages, or live heat data. Unnamed mapped links are coloured but have no added name label. Additional garden paths and actual resident walking routes require a site survey or consented GPS traces before they can be represented as known paths.

The v2 priority sidebar shows four factors in a two-by-two grid: relative walkers, sun exposure, vulnerable users, and crossing gap, followed by their product and the selected street's rank. At 1 PM, Jalan Elmina Ilham 3 uses 120 walkers per hour against a 120-per-hour reference, 0.95 sun exposure, 0.95 vulnerable-user weight, and 0.90 crossing-gap weight, giving a score of 0.81. These are planning inputs, not observed counts or measurements. For other mapped streets, the earlier relative street weight is split evenly across the four factors to preserve its approximate priority; those factor values are not independently evidenced. The selected hour scales the walker factor. The budget popup accepts RM10,000 to RM300,000 for the selected street. Proceed saves that street's amount in this browser, plays a circular network animation adapted from the local IBM Datathon frontend, and shows an itemised improvement plan. The result now compares three fixed options: Plan A has 12 young trees and site checks for RM34,000; Plan B has three shade sails and site checks for RM55,000; Plan C has eight larger nursery trees, one sail, a raised crossing and site checks for RM125,000. Each option shows its advantages, drawbacks and timing, and choices above the available budget cannot be selected. The chosen option controls the included works, remaining budget, saved plan and Ilham 3 preview. These are planning allowances, not supplier quotations or an engineering bill of quantities.

The v2 budget result offers a 3D preview for Jalan Elmina Ilham 3 when a fixed plan is selected. The Preview changes in 3D button opens `map-3d.html?preview=ilham3` with the chosen budget and plan ID. That view starts level, tilts its camera, loads one existing intermediate-house GLB, extrudes neighbouring Ilham and Frekuensi house outlines at a uniform 6 m visual height, and draws the included trees, sail, or crossing as simple 3D objects. Two approximate roof units nearest the GLB are omitted to leave its frontage clear. The house is provisionally placed at longitude 101.51837, latitude 3.18566 with an approximate orientation; the other objects use evenly spaced road-centre interpolations and a fixed northward offset. None of these placements or the house roof outline has been verified against satellite imagery or surveyed frontage. The 3D view fills the map area without a side panel; markers can be clicked for details, and Back to map returns to the v2 map. The 3D geometry is a placement study, not an impact calculation or an approved construction layout.

The eight-tree Plan C planting mix is four Bunga Tanjung (*Mimusops elengi*), two Kayu Manis hutan (*Cinnamomum iners*), and two Bungor Raya (*Lagerstroemia speciosa*). The 2026 [Jabatan Landskap Negara tree price schedule](https://elandskap.jln.gov.my/storage/uploads/epact/dokumen/1772691531702_JKH%202026.pdf) lists standard-tree unit prices of RM320, RM380, and RM350 respectively, totalling RM2,740 for eight standard trees before street-specific planting work; this does not price the larger nursery stock in Plan C. An [iGreen Nursery listing](https://www.mudah.my/bunga-tanjung-mimusops-elengi-115256497.htm) advertises 2 m Bunga Tanjung at RM200 each with a minimum order of 20; that offer cannot be applied directly to this eight-tree mix. Plan A's RM24,000 and Plan C's RM40,000 tree allowances cover different nursery stock sizes, planting pits, soil, supports and establishment. The larger-stock price is an unverified planning allowance; quantities and siting need a utility and verge survey. Each RM15,000 sail allowance uses [Coolaroo Malaysia's 5 m × 3 m commercial fabric price of RM1,550](https://coolaroomalaysia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Catalogue-with-pricing-2024.pdf) and its separate [post, footing, hardware and installation guide](https://coolaroomalaysia.com/catalogue/Coolaroo%20Sunshade%202024.pdf) as references. The RM60,000 crossing allowance is based on the [Selangor government's 2020 RM50,000 road-hump benchmark](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/pembinaan-bonggol-jalan-untuk-jalan-jalan-jkr/) for a 7 m road with signs and markings; a raised crossing may need further works. The RM10,000 site allowance covers design and utility checks and contingency. Species suitability, sail structure, crossing geometry, permissions, and actual procurement prices require site and supplier review. The same three fixed plan options are displayed for each selected street; they are not a street-specific optimisation from measured conditions.

The Resident routes layer is shown by default and draws four repeatable GPS-like lines around each selected OSM footpath beside Jalan Frekuensi U16/138 and U16/110 and through the park loop near U16/116 and U16/119. `v2-resident-routes.mjs` samples the centre line every 3 m, estimates the local tangent from points 2 m ahead and behind, and displaces each sample along the perpendicular with two smooth sine waves plus a small lane offset. The displacement tapers to zero at the ends so mapped junctions stay aligned. Eight lighter, generated shortcut traces cross between points on the mapped loop near the play area. The lines are generated visual route patterns, not recorded trips, resident counts, or evidence of footpath access. The winding interior garden paths visible in satellite imagery are not digitized from Google imagery.

Selecting a red priority pin or its offscreen indicator opens the street detail panel. The selected location moves to the center of the remaining visible map area, to the left of the panel on desktop and above it on narrow screens. The same camera placement works on the Street and Satellite basemaps.

At 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM, the map displays long directional shadows at 50% opacity. It uses the calculated solar azimuth and a minimum 5 degree elevation at these two endpoints to keep the shapes visible. If the calculated sun is below the horizon, this endpoint display is a low-sun visualization rather than a physical direct-sun shadow. From 8:00 AM through 6:00 PM, the shadows use the calculated elevation without this floor and appear at 76% opacity.

Sky tone is applied by default to the OpenStreetMap raster for the selected hour: cool and dim at 7 AM, gently warm at 8-9 AM, neutral from 10 AM through 3 PM, increasingly golden at 4-6 PM, and cool and dim at 7 PM. It changes map color only, not sun position, shadows, street priority, or Google satellite imagery. The palette follows general photography guidance on golden-hour warmth and blue-hour cool tones; it does not represent measured sky or weather conditions. Sources: [Adobe on golden and blue hour](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography/technique/golden-hour.html), [Nikon on early and late light](https://www.nikonusa.com/learn-and-explore/c/tips-and-techniques/tips-for-photographing-mountains), [MapLibre raster paint properties](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-style-spec/layers/).

The v2 map starts with the OpenStreetMap Street map. Its optional Satellite switch uses Google Maps JavaScript satellite imagery on localhost, with the OpenStreetMap road and selected building footprints and the same calculated shadows drawn above it. Satellite images were captured on dates independent of the selected shadow time, so shadows already visible in the photograph may differ. The Street map remains the MapLibre view with the 2.5D tilt; Google satellite is top-down here. Google supplies the imagery and its attribution, while the road and building geometry comes from the OpenStreetMap snapshot. The local server reads `TEDUH_GOOGLE_MAPS_KEY` from the Windows user environment and never writes it to the repository. A Maps Demo Key supports this local Google Maps JavaScript view but not Map Tiles API.

The 3D view uses MapLibre and OpenFreeMap vector tiles for the basemap. Outside the Ilham and Frekuensi area, building extrusions use the project's OpenStreetMap footprint snapshot with a uniform 5 m visual height because that snapshot has no building heights. In the Ilham and Frekuensi area, the mapped and road-aligned approximate roof units from `v2-houses.mjs` are extruded at 6 m; their source footprints are omitted to prevent stacked geometry. Five adjacent mapped terrace footprints near Jalan Metafasa U16/163 use original GLB house models, one end unit and four intermediate units. Their facades, side yard, and heights are visual approximations, not verified designs of those homes or of homes on Jalan Elmina Ilham 3. The models are scaled to the selected footprints and replace their generic extrusions. Modular sidewalk GLBs follow those five frontages, with tree-well modules at alternating houses and a side-yard extension at the end unit. Straight road GLBs follow the nearby mapped road centerline. Their 3 m sidewalk and 6 m road widths are visual assumptions; OpenStreetMap does not provide surveyed curb positions here. A visual verge with separate driveway strips fills the gap between the modeled sidewalk and mapped road strip; its layout is not surveyed. Intersections and bends still use the basemap. Trees, sails, crossings, and lights are georeferenced representations of saved proposed fixes, not surveyed designs. The view flags saved points that overlap mapped building footprints; review those placements before using them in a plan. The 3D view reads the same browser-local proposed fixes and reports as the 2D map and refreshes while open.

This GitHub Pages site has no backend. Proposed map fixes are stored in the current browser and can appear across the 2D and 3D views. The report flow inside `sdp-app.html` keeps new reports in that page until reload. Neither flow submits to Sime Darby Property or a council. The separate private pilot can use a shared server and database.

The map-first workspace has Proposed changes and Reports panels over the map. Saving a budget result stores the included works as a street plan in this browser (`teduh-v2-plans-v1`), with suggested points along the selected mapped street. The planner can adjust the budget, inspect each work, set its map location, change its status, or remove the plan. The site-check allowance has no pin. These plans are separate from the older map's `teduh-team-local-v1` proposal records and do not automatically appear in the 3D view. The Reports panel reads that older browser-local report store, shows map pins and status filters, and can save a status and reply. The owner-app phone page currently keeps new reports in its own page memory, so those reports do not yet reach this panel. The browser-local API accepts report coordinates across the Ilham and Frekuensi map area, east to longitude 101.5221. None of these records is shared across devices.

The resident report list also includes two image-backed entries carried over from the former planner. They are labelled as earlier planner entries, and their images as generated illustrations rather than resident photos. Their status and team notes can be updated in this browser (`teduh-v2-legacy-report-updates-v1`). The shade entry appears as a report-style marker on Jalan Elmina Ilham 3 and below that street's priority formula, but its exact point is unconfirmed and it does not affect the score. The crossing entry retains its earlier Persiaran Elmina name without a map pin because that location has not been matched to the current Ilham and Frekuensi view. The older planner's support counts, dates, and Planned/Built claims are not imported as resident activity. A new browser also receives one proposed RM110,000 plan on Jalan Frekuensi U16/120, with RM95,000 in included works and RM15,000 unallocated. Its suggested pins require site review. The plan is added once and is not restored after removal.

The map also shows planning concern markers on Jalan Frekuensi U16/113 and U16/120. Their notes prompt review of shade and crossing access; they are not submitted resident reports, and they do not affect the priority formula. Marker positions come from the mapped street geometry rather than verified issue locations.

The dark street-priority panel ranks named mapped streets by the same score shown in each street's formula. Each bar spans 0 to 1, so its length represents the absolute score rather than a share of the highest-ranked street. The list reorders with the time slider. Ilham 3 and Frekuensi U16/113 and U16/120 have distinct assumed hourly walking profiles in `v2-street-priority.mjs`; the other streets use the common hourly profile. The edge badges appear only after their associated map pins leave the viewport.

Serve the repository through a local HTTP server when testing. Opening the HTML files through `file://` prevents map data requests from loading. No build step is required.
For the v2 Satellite view, run `python v2-local.py` and open `http://127.0.0.1:8765/v2-map.html`. Restart the local server after changing the Windows user key. The GitHub Pages version has no access to the local key, so Satellite is unavailable there.

## Team workflow

Pull the latest `main` before editing, test the changed screen and connected flow, then commit and push. Keep the page, script, and presentation values aligned. The site is published from this repository through GitHub Pages.
